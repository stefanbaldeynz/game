# Levels from other games (only on your computer)

These files change how levels are built. Each is made from a game you have installed, by a script in `C:\Users\Stefan\bfme\tools`.

| File | What it does | Made with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `oakvale.json` | Turns Hobbiton into **Oakvale**, the village from the start of Fable: The Lost Chapters. You get its hillside down to the sea, and the buildings where Fable puts them: houses, tavern, shops and the trader's bridge. Its fences, walls, rocks, lamps, crates and chickens come too, and trees, bushes, crops and flowers grow wherever Fable's ground says they do. The nine Nazgûl hunt you through it as usual. | `Prep-Oakvale.ps1` |
| `rivendell.json` | Builds **Rivendell** in the river gorge from Fable's Greatwood. The Bruinen runs deep down a ravine with high wooded banks either side, and two bridges cross high over the water. The Last Homely House, the houses, towers and gazebo stand on the level ground along the edge of the gorge, looking down on the river. Falls pour where the river drops fastest, and Fable's old walls become elven ruins. | `Prep-Oakvale.ps1 -Region Greatwood_4 -Out C:\Users\Stefan\game\valinor\levels\rivendell.json -Keep '^OBJECT_(WOODEN_BRIDGE\|BW_BRIDGE\|BRIGHTWOOD_.*ROCK\|OK_WALL\|OK_PILLAR\|GREATWOOD_TREESTUMP)'` |
| `ut/gondolin.json` (and its pictures in `textures/ut/`) | Builds **Gondolin** from Unreal Tournament 2004's own stonework, rocks and trees. Crossfire's and the Barrens' pillars, walls and round towers become the white city, re-toned to marble with gold. The gates' pillars, the statues on the Great Stair and the braziers are UT2004 pieces too. Arborea's cliffs line the ravine of the Seven Gates, and Antalus's and Arborea's trees grow on Tumladen. Without it, Gondolin is built from plain shapes. | `pwsh Prep-UTKit.ps1 -Kit gondolin -Packages NaThAncientPack, BarrenHardware, Albatross_architecture, ArboreaHardware, ArboreaLanscape, AntalusStatic` (also with UCC). To see a kit's pieces, open `valinor/tools/kitview.html?kit=gondolin`. |
| `ut/rivendell.json` | Builds **Elrond's house** in Rivendell as the great hall of CTF-MoonDragon. It uses the Far East's red-lacquered wall panels and shoji doors, its balcony rails and its bronze urns. They stand in three storeys under jade roofs, with paper lanterns at the eaves and along the bridges. | `pwsh Prep-UTKit.ps1 -Kit rivendell -Packages far` (UCC stops part-way through the Far East package, so only its first 14 pieces come across) |
| `ut/bridgeoffate.json` | Builds **the Mines of Moria** on the whole of CTF-BridgeOfFate. The halls are carved from the map's brushes as UnrealEd does it and lit by the map's own lights. Its columns, arches, banners and fire pots are where the map has them. You start in its great base hall and fight through four of its halls, which the game picks from the floor you can walk to on foot. | `Prep-UTLevel.ps1 -Map CTF-BridgeOfFate -Name bridgeoffate`. It uses UCC for the layout and UE Viewer (`C:\Users\Stefan\bfme\umodel`) for the meshes and pictures. Add `&utreach=3` to the address to see a map of the floors you can reach. |
| `mirkwood.json` | Builds **Mirkwood** on DM-TokaraForest from Unreal Tournament 2004: its forest bowl, its giant trees and branches, and its catwalks and ramps. Valinor rebuilds them in bark and old planks, and you can walk the walkways. Tokara's hanging lights become Morgul lanterns, its pods become egg sacs, its jump pads become web springs, and its bot paths are where the spiders start. | `Prep-Tokara.ps1` (it runs UT2004's own `UCC.exe` to take the map apart) |

- For Oakvale, Rivendell and Mirkwood, the ground and where everything stands come from those games. Their buildings and plants are Battle for Middle-earth's own when `models/props/` has them (see its README), and Valinor's own shapes otherwise. The `ut/` kits bring Unreal Tournament 2004's own 3D pieces and pictures across.
- To play the built-in levels while these files are here, add `?layout=shire` (Hobbiton) or `?layout=classic` (Rivendell, Mirkwood, Gondolin and the Mines) to the address, or delete the file. Without `mirkwood.json`, Mirkwood grows a smaller forest bowl of its own.

**Everything in this folder stays on your computer.** Git ignores it (only this README is in the repository). Fable belongs to Microsoft and Lionhead, and Unreal Tournament 2004 to Epic, so keep these for playing at home.
