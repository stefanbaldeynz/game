# Fire from Battle for Middle-earth (only on your computer)

Helm's Deep's Balrog uses Battle for Middle-earth's own fire when its pictures are in this folder:

- **Flames:** sheets of frames that play as each flame rises and fades.
- **Fireballs:** in the rain of fire and as it lands.
- **The blast:** rolls out as a shockwave.

Without them, the game uses its own flames.

To fill it, with BFME II / Rise of the Witch-king installed, run:

```powershell
& C:\Users\Stefan\bfme\tools\Prep-Fx.ps1
```

It doesn't need Blender: it takes the pictures straight out of the game's archives and saves them as `.png`.

**Everything in this folder stays on your computer.** Git ignores it (only this README is in the repository). The pictures belong to EA, so keep them for playing at home.
