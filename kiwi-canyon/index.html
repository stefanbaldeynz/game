Kiwi Canyon

Waves of kiwis are marching through the canyon. Build towers on the rim, upgrade them, and jump down into the canyon with your rifle to help. Don't let them through!

  • Tower view: click the grassy rim to build a tower. Click a tower to upgrade it, up to 3 times.
  • F jumps you into the canyon in first person. F or Esc takes you back up.
  • Tower view: tap the grassy rim to build a tower. Tap a tower to upgrade it, up to 3 times. Drag to look around, pinch to zoom.
  • First-person mode drops you into the canyon: left thumb moves (push far to sprint), drag the right side to look, FIRE to shoot. Tower view takes you back up.
  • Every kiwi you stop earns $1, plus a bonus for each wave you clear.
  • 10 waves. Every wave the kiwis get tougher. Each kiwi that escapes costs a life.
